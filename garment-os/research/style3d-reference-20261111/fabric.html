FAQ

Q: What is Style3D Fabric?
A: Style3D Fabric helps digitize textiles into realistic 3D digital materials. It captures visual texture and physical behavior for accurate garment simulation, and enables digital material editing.
Q: Which fabric data matters most?
A: Texture, color, thickness, stretch, shear, bending, and drape all affect digital fabric realism. Accurate data helps 3D garments look and move more like real products.
Q: How do mills use digital fabrics?
A: Mills can present digital fabric libraries to brands for faster sourcing and virtual sampling. This helps buyers review materials earlier without waiting for every physical swatch.
